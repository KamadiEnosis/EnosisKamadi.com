About

A career built across clinic, classroom and community

My work spans clinical nutrition practice, dietetics, and public health research, alongside a parallel career training the next generation of nutrition and dietetics professionals under Kenya's CBET/CDACC framework. I currently deliver training in Level 6(Diploma) and Level 5 (Certificate) in Nutrition and Dietetics, and I Hold a BSc in Human Nutrition and Dietetics from Kenya Methodist University and a Pedagogy from Kenya School of TVET, registered with the Kenya Nutritionists and Dietitians Institute (KNDI).

My clinical grounding comes from Internship/attachments at county referral and sub-county hospitals, where I worked directly on malnutrition management, nutrition care planning, and patient counselling et.al,. That clinical lens now shapes everything I build, from the curriculum materials I develop for trainee dietitians to the digital tools I design for people across the continent.

Beyond the clinic and classroom, I write on health, environment, and human-social behaviour, and I champion digital health and AI as tools for closing Africa's nutrition and mental-health service gaps, while mentoring peers and advocating for the wellbeing of people living with mental and psychosocial conditions.

Founder spotlight

NutriPulse — nutrition intelligence for Africa

NutriPulse is an AI-powered digital nutrition platform I founded to close the personalized-nutrition gap across Africa. It combines a growing pan-African food database, personalized meal planning and health tracking, built around the realities of diverse diets, budgets and lifestyles from Nairobi to Africa and Beyond.

The platform reflects everything I care about professionally: clinical rigor, cultural relevance, and technology that actually reaches the people who need it — wherever they are on the continent.
